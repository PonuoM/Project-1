# Competitors: idle / incremental / desktop-companion games with Path of Exile-style build depth (as of 14 Oct 2026)

Scope note: research was done on 2026-10-14. Steam prices are shown in THB because the store localized to Thailand. Review counts change daily and different trackers report different snapshots, so conflicts are flagged. Reddit could not be fetched directly (reddit.com blocked for the fetch tool), so player sentiment comes from Steam reviews, itch.io comments, review aggregators and press.

## Q1. Which idle / incremental / auto-battle ARPGs copy Path of Exile or Diablo-like itemization, and which POE systems do they use?

### Takeaway
The "idle Path of Exile" idea is not new. At least 10 titles on Steam, web/itch.io and Android already pitch themselves as an idle or incremental take on PoE/Diablo, starting with Godsbane Idle (2022). Most are small (under 500 reviews), unreleased, or in alpha. The best-funded ones that say "PoE" outright (Exile: Idle ARPG, Lootlands) are not out yet (Q1 2027 / "coming soon"). None of the released titles I found has been shown to combine all three core PoE pillars: linked support gems, a very large passive tree, and orb/currency crafting of random affixes.

Systems listed: active skills + **Support Gems**, elemental paths, Legendary Powers; rares, high-tier affixes, crafted upgrades; Rift tiers with bosses; leaderboards. The store copy says "Diablo Rifts. Path of Exile Crafting." No passive tree, ascendancy, trade, maps or desktop/taskbar mode is mentioned. ### Inferences
- The user's belief that "nobody has done this yet" is **false as a general claim**. "Idle Path of Exile" has been tried repeatedly since at least 2022: Godsbane Idle, Idle Exile, Path of Idling, Endless Exile, Grind to Rust, Pixel Valkyrie, Exile: Idle ARPG and Lootlands.
- The claim **holds in a narrower form**. No released, commercially significant title was found that delivers full PoE-level depth (6-link-style support gems + huge passive tree + orb crafting of random affixes + trade) in an idle format. The released attempts are tiny, free or ad-supported, or alpha. Godsbane Idle, the oldest Steam one, sits at "Mixed."
- Exile: Idle ARPG (Q1 2027) and Lootlands are the **most direct future competitors**. Both use "Path of Exile" in their marketing, and both should be watched (wishlists, playtests).

## Q2. Desktop idle / taskbar "idler while you work" games on Steam: which have deep RPG build systems, and how successful are they?

### Takeaway
The desktop-companion niche boomed in 2025–2026. Bongo Cat peaked around 190k concurrent players, and TBH: Task Bar Hero peaked at about 526–546k, although bots and Steam-Market farming inflated that number. A whole wave of **desktop ARPG/loot idlers** now exists: Loot of Baal, Desktop Heroes, Desktop Raid, TBH, Deskrawl, Lootborne, Duelist, Taskbar Quest and Task Bar Cube. Several already have random affixes, talent trees and **Steam Market trading**. Their build depth is Diablo-2-like at best, and reviewers repeatedly call builds restrictive or loot "stat sticks." None advertises PoE-style linked support gems, a giant passive tree or orb crafting.

### Inferences
- The "desktop widget + ARPG loot + online trade" slot is **already occupied and crowded**. Deskrawl (42k peak, Sep 2026), TBH (500k+ peak, F2P + Steam Market) and Loot of Baal (about 1.4k reviews, online trade) prove the demand. A new entrant cannot claim to be the first desktop ARPG idler.
- **Gamersky Games** co-publishes Loot of Baal, Desktop Raid and Deskrawl, so a Chinese publisher appears to be running a pipeline of desktop idle ARPGs. Rusty's Retirement's 26% China sales share also suggests the Chinese market matters for this niche.
- Their depth is the gap. Their systems are classes + skills + random affixes + sets/runes (Diablo 2 / Diablo 3 level). None advertises linked support gems, a passive tree of hundreds of nodes, or deterministic/stochastic currency crafting. A desktop idler with genuine PoE-style theorycrafting would still be **differentiated on depth, not on format**.
- Steam Market integration is a double-edged growth engine (see Q4). It drives virality, as with TBH, but it attracts bots and RMT, and Valve load limits apply.

## Q3. Mobile idle RPGs with deep, PoE-like builds

### Takeaway
On mobile, PoE-like depth exists in **non-idle** ARPGs (Undecember with skill/link runes and a Zodiac passive board; Torchlight: Infinite). The only idle title found with PoE-pattern systems is the small, ad-supported **Path of Idling** on Android. Mainstream mobile idle RPGs (AFK Journey, IdleOn, Passive Master) are gacha/loot-box or MMO-style, not PoE-style crafting. Path of Exile Mobile was announced in 2019, and I found no evidence it has been released.

### Cited Findings
- **Path of Idling** (Android): passive perks tree with 300+ nodes, active + support skills, map and gear crafting with parameters, offline progress, ad-supported. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.SmoothDev.PathofIdling&hl=en); [APKPure](https://apkpure.net/path-of-idling-idle-rpg/com.SmoothDev.PathofIdling)
- **Undecember** (LINE Games / Needs Games; Korea launch Jan 2022, global 2022; mobile + PC). Classless. **Skill Runes** are hexagons with up to six colored sides. **Link Runes** attach only when colors match (green DEX, blue INT, red STR), much like PoE's colored gem links. The **Zodiac** board is the passive-tree equivalent. — [PocketGamer runes/zodiac guide](https://www.pocketgamer.com/undecember/runes-zodiac-points-gears-guide); [Gfinity first impressions](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/undecember-first-impressions); [LINE Games press release](https://gamespress.com/LINE-Games-Hack-Slash-ARPG-UNDECEMBER-Reaches-3-Million-Pre-registrati)
- **Torchlight: Infinite** (XD Inc.). F2P on mobile + PC, seasonal (e.g. "Cube of Rapacity"), talents. Common Sense Media criticizes it: managing loot and progression is "a slog through layers of menus", and those menus "aren't very informative." Early PC testers complained it felt like a mobile UI with no mouse-hover info. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/torchlight-infinite-goes-cross-platform-launching-worldwide-on-mobile-and-pc); [Common Sense Media](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/torchlight-infinite); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1974050/discussions/0/5254037276307385144)
- **Path of Exile Mobile**: announced at ExileCon 2019 as an in-house "experimental" version, promising no ads, microtransactions or energy bars. No release date was found in any source. — [PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/path-of-exile-mobile/path-of-exile-mobile-release-date-and-the-rest-you-need-to-know/); [TouchArcade](https://toucharcade.com/2019/11/18/path-of-exile-mobile-trailer-announced-exilecon-ios-android-2019/)

### Inferences
- A **PoE-depth idle game for mobile** has, in practice, only been attempted by tiny indies (Path of Idling). Undecember shows that PoE-style color-linked "rune" gems can be made touch-friendly. Torchlight: Infinite shows the UI risk: deep menus on a phone feel like a slog.
- None of the desktop-companion ARPGs in Q2 lists a mobile version, so "desktop now, mobile later with the same account/economy" appears unclaimed among the bigger titles.

## Q4. What do players praise or criticize, and why do these games fail?

### Takeaway
Players love "Diablo 2 for busy people": background play on a second monitor, low resource use, constant loot. They punish five things: (1) bad UI and inventory management, especially in a tiny window; (2) **restrictive/shallow builds and "stat-stick" loot**; (3) **real-money / Steam Market economies** that bring bots, RMT and pay-to-win ladders; (4) server instability in online-required idlers; (5) progress that is too slow or needs too much attention for an "idle" game. Small indie PoE-idle attempts also suffer from stalled alpha development and thin itemization.

### Cited Findings
**Deskrawl (Steam reviews, Sep–Oct 2026)**
- Praise: "Diablo 2 for busy people"; fun to leave open on another monitor; "vast" talent trees with "a ton of skills"; good value; Patch 1.0.2 doubled super-rare drop rates in single-player. — [Steam reviews API](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/4623570?json=1&language=english&filter=all&num_per_page=40&purchase_type=all)
- Criticism: the most common complaint is the Steam Market trading, which "creates this very Diablo 3 predatory real money auction house dynamic"; leaderboards feel meaningless "when the best items can be bought outright with real money"; "Builds are extremely restrictive"; "Loot is rare, terrible, and generally just long stat sticks"; "Hunter is an unfinished mess"; window starts below the taskbar; players want co-op. — [Steam reviews API](https://store.steampowered.com/appreviews/4623570?json=1&language=english&filter=all&num_per_page=40&purchase_type=all)

**Loot of Baal (VaporLens analysis of Steam reviews)**
- Positives by mention count: idle/AFK multitasking (93), Diablo-like loot farming (48), equipment system with crafting, gem synthesis, sets and rerolls (34), pixel art (28), online trading (21), low resource use. — [VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3358250/loot_of_baal)
- Negatives: UI/UX and display options (95: no fullscreen or resize, tiny UI, eye strain; fullscreen requested 54 times; some refunds); inventory management (95: no auto-sell, weak filter, convenience locked behind progression); connectivity and server instability (77: disconnects also break offline saves); character customization (76: skills too random, small pool, limited affixes); slow progression that requires attention (75). A player warns that a misconfigured loot filter can destroy items forever. — [VaporLens](https://vaporlens.app/app/3358250/loot_of_baal); [search summary of reviews](https://www.incrementaldb.com/community/review/5523)

**TBH: Task Bar Hero (press coverage)**
- Bots farm items to sell on the Steam Market: over 200,000 wood listings, and rare weapons listed above $1,000 (one bow listed at $1,585.75 whose highest actual sale was $22). ### Inferences
- **Depth complaints cut both ways.** Desktop ARPG idlers are criticized as too shallow ("restrictive builds", "stat sticks", "limited affixes"), which supports the user's thesis that PoE-level depth is wanted. At the same time, the top complaint category is UI/inventory, so PoE-level depth in a small widget is a hard UI problem. Loot filters, auto-sell, stash tabs and a resizable/fullscreen "management mode" look essential.
- **Online trade via Steam Market is the riskiest feature.** It produced TBH's viral peak, but also bots, RMT, Valve load limits, false bans and Mixed reviews, and Deskrawl reviewers attack the same dynamic. An in-game trade system with anti-bot design (or SSF/league options, as in Grind to Rust's SSF mode) is safer.
- An "idle" game whose progress needs constant attention gets punished (Loot of Baal, Godsbane's "no offline progress").

## Q5. Is there a clear gap (e.g. desktop widget + PoE-depth crafting + online trade), or is it already occupied?

### Takeaway
The format is **not** a gap: desktop-companion ARPG idlers with loot, affixes and online/Steam-Market trading already exist and are commercially proven (Deskrawl, TBH, Loot of Baal, Desktop Raid). The remaining gap is **depth plus platform combination**. As of 14 Oct 2026 I found no released game that combines (a) an always-on desktop/taskbar widget, (b) genuine PoE-style build depth (linked support gems + large passive tree + currency/orb crafting of random affixes), (c) a healthy player economy, and (d) a linked mobile version. The window is closing: Exile: Idle ARPG (Q1 2027) and Lootlands ("What if Path of Exile played itself?") are in playtest, and desktop-ARPG releases arrive almost monthly.

### Cited Findings
- Desktop + ARPG + trade already exists: Deskrawl (Steam Market trading of gear, materials and minions; 42k peak), TBH (Steam Market; 500k+ peak), Loot of Baal (online item trading). — [Steam: Deskrawl](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4623570/Deskrawl_Idle_ARPG/); [Raijin: Deskrawl](https://raijin.gg/app/4623570/Deskrawl_Idle_ARPG); [Steam: TBH](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3678970/TBH_Task_Bar_Hero/); [Steam: Loot of Baal](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3358250/Loot_of_Baal/)
- None of those three store pages mentions skill gems/supports, a passive tree or currency crafting. Deskrawl's page lists gear, minions and abilities; TBH lists a "Cube System" for item stats; Loot of Baal lists runes, affixes and synthesis. — same sources as above.
- The PoE-depth idle games are not desktop widgets: Exile: Idle ARPG (support gems, affixes, crafting; no desktop mode mentioned; Q1 2027), Lootlands (talent tree, crafting; coming soon), Pixel Valkyrie (350+ node tree, skill gems; active ARPG), Path of Idling (300+ node tree, support skills; Android, ad-supported). — [Exile: Idle ARPG](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5161980/Exile_Idle_ARPG/); [Lootlands](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3397910/Lootlands_Idle_ARPG/); [Pixel Valkyrie](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4258830/Pixel_Valkyrie/); [Path of Idling](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.SmoothDev.PathofIdling&hl=en)
- The desktop-companion catalog is growing fast: wasdland lists 36+ titles released Nov 2024 to Sep 2026, and ARPG-type idlers launched in Mar 2026 (Desktop Raid), May 2026 (TBH), Sep 2026 (Lootborne, Deskrawl) and Oct 2026 (Taskbar Quest, Task Bar Cube). — [wasdland](https://www.wasdland.com/games/desktop-companion/); [search summary of Steam listings](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5286430)

### Inferences
- **An honest verdict on "nobody has done this":**
  - Wrong if it means "idle game inspired by PoE" (Godsbane Idle 2022, Path of Idling, Idle Exile, Grind to Rust, Endless Exile) or "desktop-widget ARPG with loot and trade" (Deskrawl, TBH, Loot of Baal).
  - Mostly right if it means a polished desktop widget with real PoE-level build depth (support-gem links + big passive tree + orb crafting) and a mobile companion. No released product matches all of that, although Exile: Idle ARPG may come close on depth in Q1 2027. Its Steam page does not mention a desktop/taskbar mode.
- Differentiation therefore has to come from: (1) build depth that desktop rivals lack, since reviewers already complain about restrictive builds and stat-stick loot; (2) a UI that makes that depth manageable in widget form, the #1 complaint across the genre; (3) a trade design that avoids TBH/Deskrawl's real-money-auction-house and bot problems; (4) desktop ⇄ mobile cross-progression, which none of the desktop rivals advertises.
- Speed matters. Exile: Idle ARPG and Lootlands target the same "PoE that plays itself" pitch, and the Gamersky Games pipeline (Loot of Baal → Desktop Raid → Deskrawl) suggests well-resourced studios are iterating quickly in this exact niche.
