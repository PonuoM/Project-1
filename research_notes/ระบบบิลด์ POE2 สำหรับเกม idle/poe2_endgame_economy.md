# Path of Exile 2 — Endgame, League Mechanics, Player-Trade Economy & Development Timeline (state as of 14 Oct 2026)

> Research date: 2026-10-14. Live version at time of writing: **0.5.x "Return of the Ancients"** with the **Runes of Aldur** challenge league, plus the **0.5.5 "Forbidden Rites"** event league (started 4 Sep 2026). **1.0 full release is scheduled for 11 Dec 2026** (not yet live).
> Source-quality legend: [OFFICIAL] = pathofexile.com; [GUIDE] = Maxroll / wikis / fan guides; [PRESS] = news outlets; [COMMERCIAL] = sites that sell currency/boosting (e.g. timesaver.gg, aoeah.com) — they have a stake in the economy, so treat their numbers as indicative only; [COMMUNITY] = forum/Steam posts (anecdotal).
> "OUTDATED" marks information that applied to an earlier patch.

## 1. Development timeline, league cadence, player-count trends

### Takeaway
POE2 entered paid Early Access on 6 Dec 2024. It shipped 4 major content updates in about 18 months (0.2 Apr 2025, 0.3 Aug 2025, 0.4 Dec 2025, 0.5 May 2026), each with a new challenge league and a fresh economy, so the cadence works out to roughly one big league every 3.5–5.5 months. A mid-cycle event league (0.5.5) also started in Sep 2026. There will be no 0.6, and 1.0 (free-to-play, Duelist class, final 2 acts) lands on 11 Dec 2026. Steam concurrency follows a sharp "spike and decay" pattern: each league launch reaches 245k–420k concurrent players, then falls 80–97% within 2–3 months.

### Inferences
- League cadence ≈ every 4–5 months for major content: Dec→Apr (4 mo), Apr→Aug (~5 mo), Aug→Dec (3.5 mo), Dec→May (5.5 mo), plus an event league about 3 months after 0.5. That is slower than POE1's roughly 13-week rhythm. The economy reset on each league is the main "come back" trigger.
- Retention shape: peak-to-trough drops of about 95%+ within 3 months (e.g. Apr 2026 avg 6.9k vs Jun 2026 avg 173k). The playerbase is "seasonal binge" rather than "daily habit", which is the opposite of an idle game's goal. For an idle game, the transferable idea is **periodic resets with fresh-economy hype** (seasons), not POE2's retention curve.
- Each league adds one mechanic that later goes "core" into the Atlas (Abyss → core, Vaal → core, Runes of Aldur → core in 0.5.5). An idle game can copy this: a season-limited mechanic that, if popular, becomes a permanent system.

### Gaps
- No official GGG statement on future league length/cadence post-1.0 was found.
- Total sales and revenue figures were not found. Wikipedia gives none.
- October 2026 player data was unavailable (trackers last updated ~22 Sep 2026).
- 0.1.x minor patch details (e.g. 0.1.1 changes to maps and death) were not researched.

## 2. Endgame structure (Atlas, Waystones, Towers/Tablets, Fortress, Atlas tree, pinnacle bosses, league content)

### Takeaway
In 0.5 the endgame changed from an endless, procedurally revealed Atlas grid into a **structured set of questlines**. Players start with Precursor Towers, which spawn a Fortress; Fortress maps are the source of Atlas passive points, and the Arbiter of Ash and Arbiter of Divinity are the main pinnacles. Each league mechanic (Breach, Delirium, Ritual, Expedition, Abyss, Vaal, Runes of Aldur) now has a fixed hub, a questline ending in a pinnacle boss, and a "quest version vs infinite farm version" split. Waystones (T1–T15, T16 via corruption) remain the consumable entry keys. Tablets and Waystone mods are the "juice" knobs that trade risk for reward.

### Cited Findings
**Access and Atlas basics**
- Mapping unlocks by entering the Vaal Ziggurat and talking to Doryani, around character level 65 after the 3 interludes. — [Maxroll Atlas guide](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- Players put a Waystone into the Map Device at the Ziggurat Refuge to open a randomized map. 0.5 added fixed points of interest: each league mechanic has a permanent hub and a quest leading to its pinnacle. Progression has 3 strands: the Precursor Fortress (Atlas points), league mechanics (farming) and master missions. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide)
- Special Atlas encounters include Powerful Map Bosses, Precursor Towers (reveal a large area), Beast Corruption (corrupted modifiers), Hideouts, **Moment of Zen** (one free unique item), Citadels (special boss encounters) and special maps such as **Untainted Paradise** (large XP). — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- The 0.5 design goal, per one guide, is to give players "a clearer stopping point" through 5 distinct storylines instead of an endless Waystone loop. — [games.gg](https://games.gg/path-of-exile-2/guides/poe-2-patch-05-return-of-the-ancients-rundown/)

**Waystones**
- Waystones are single-use, craftable entry keys dropped by monsters and endgame activities. They start at **Tier I**; higher-level monsters can drop the next tier, up to **XV**, and empowered map bosses always drop higher tiers. **XVI** is obtainable only by corrupting a XV with a **Vaal Orb**. Tier colours are I–V white, VI–X yellow and XI–XV red, with XIV–XVI described as especially rewarding. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- Each explicit modifier raises monster difficulty, pack size, monster effectiveness, rarity and loot quantity. Crafted Waystones can have **up to 6 modifiers**; Tablets can have **up to 4**. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping). Prefixes increase rewards and suffixes increase danger. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide)
- Since 0.5.0, "Waystones must now be identified in order to activate them in the Map Device." Over 40 new **Ancient Modifiers** can appear on maps outside the Fortress. Omens of Chaotic Rarity, Quantity and Monsters now *prevent* matching modifiers, with up to 3 Omens active at once. A new Omen of Chaotic Effectiveness replaces Waystone mods with mods that grant no Monster Effectiveness. "Monster Effectiveness now gives half the previous bonus to item Quantity and Experience." — [Official 0.5.0 patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)

**Precursor Towers and Tablets**
- Completing a Precursor Tower reveals a large area around it, and the first tower also spawns the Fortress. Tablets are added when opening a map to customize it. Tablet drops are **self-reinforcing**: using more Tablets of a mechanic yields more Tablets of that mechanic. Maxroll's changelog notes Tablets are "now used on maps instead of towers" (Oct 2025 entry). — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping). Towers and Tablets were first overhauled in 0.2.0. — [search summary of Maxroll/poe2hub](https://poe2hub.net/mechanics/atlas-waystones-towers/)
- High-end "juicing": find the right city biome, place a Grand Mirror so its radius covers the city, then roll Tablet percentages. Sustaining Tablet supply is hard, and this is described as "the most-complained-about system" despite having the highest ceiling. — [timesaver [COMMERCIAL]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)
- 0.5.5 adds **Expedition Tablets**, e.g. a tablet that spawns 2 Expeditions in one area. — [Maxroll 0.5.5](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-5-forbidden-rites-event-launch-date-and-endgame-changes)

**Fortress, Citadels, Arbiters**
- The Fortress contains Patriarch and Matriarch Halls; clearing them opens the Origin Tower, where **The Arbiter of Divinity** (the new pinnacle) waits. Fortress maps carry 40+ Ancient Modifiers, e.g. rares converted to essence monsters, packs inside strongboxes, and Azmeri spirits that don't disappear. Clearing a section auto-completes its unfinished maps. — [search summary of Maxroll/rpgstash guides](https://www.rpgstash.com/blog/poe-2-endgame-guide-return-of-the-ancients); [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary)
- Fortress maps are **the only source of Atlas passive points**. Inside, players open Gateways, reach the Burning Monolith, and clear 2 Enigma Chambers for Crisis fragments, which summon the **Arbiter of Ash** at the Burning Monolith. Citadel maps past the Fortress have bosses that drop keys for the **Arbiter of Divinity**. Each Arbiter of Divinity kill "zaps" a Fortress section, auto-completing its maps and granting points; 5 kills clear the remaining sections. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide). **Conflict:** Maxroll says to find **3 keys** inside the Fortress and then defeat the Arbiter of Ash to access the centre. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping). In **0.5.5 only 1 additional Arbiter kill** reveals the full Fortress (replacing the multi-kill requirement). — [Maxroll 0.5.5](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-5-forbidden-rites-event-launch-date-and-endgame-changes)
- The first Arbiter of Divinity encounter is a weaker version. Precursor Relays grant Atlas points, and the central section grants 1 Atlas point per map completed that way. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping). In 0.5 the old Arbiter (of Ash) got a questline, and "Fragments are easier to find." Primary, Secondary and Tertiary Calamity Fragments can no longer be obtained. — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)

**Atlas passive tree and Masters**
- The tree was fully reworked and is much larger, with "over 300 nodes", and completing all Fortress maps gives enough points to allocate every node. — [Official patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540). Maxroll counts **301** main points; another guide says **311**. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping); [search summary](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4538--poe-2-05-best-atlas-tree--map-farming-strats-return-of-the-ancients--poe-2-runes-of-aldur-atlas-guide)
- There is **no respec** except for multi-choice keystone nodes, which are swappable in the hideout (e.g. a powerful-boss map gets an extra Essence, a Summoning Circle or a Rogue Exile). 0.5.1 added 24 biome nodes. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide); [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- **Masters of the Atlas**: only 1 is active at a time, with up to 3 abilities, and switching is free between maps. Some options have downsides. **Jado** (spycraft) gives extra uniques from map bosses, a higher chance of unique strongboxes, and +1 mod on corrupted Waystones. **Hilda** (hunting) raises the chance of powerful map bosses and forces Azmeri spirits toward unique bosses. **Doryani** (science) empowers Waystones, e.g. +1 revival or higher mod effectiveness, for harder maps with better rewards. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping); [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary)

**League mechanics in the Atlas: what each rewards**
- **Breach** (hub: Monastery of the Keepers): Breachstone Splinters stack into a **Wombgift**. A progress bar turns a completed breach into a Stabilised Breach. It feeds Genesis Tree crafting, which has up to **15 skill points per tree across 4 trees (Currency, Amulets, Rings, Belts)** with free respec. **Catalysts now come only from the Genesis Tree**. It also has the Stabilised Breach boss Vruun and the Marshal of Xesht. — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide)
- **Delirium** (hub: The Withered Willow): each chained map is **10% more delirious** than the last, and the Trial of Madness can reach **200% Deliriousness**. Rewards are Distilled Emotions (Liquid Emotions add specific jewel mods, working like a Chaos Orb; Potent Emotions grant special jewel mods or amulet notables), Timelost Jewel crafting and the 7-wave Simulacrum. — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide)
- **Ritual** (hub: Caer Tarth): players earn Tribute and spend it on **Uniques and Omens**, deferring and rerolling rewards to chase high-end uniques (e.g. Mageblood, Headhunter). — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide); [timesaver [COMMERCIAL]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)
- **Expedition**: Grand Expedition questline with bosses Medved, Uhtred and Olroth, Liquid Verisium (spent at Farrow, sellable in bulk), and a 24-node Expedition Atlas tree (0.5.4). The Recombinator is disabled and Expedition is disabled on Standard during the league. — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)
- **Abyss** (league in 0.3, core since): bosses drop **Desecrated currency** (0.5.4). Abyss Omens don't drop below area level 65, and the questline requires 3 faction bosses to unlock Kulemak. — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary)
- **Fate of the Vaal** (league in 0.4, now core; hubs: Lira Vaal and Atziri's Temple): rewards are Energised Crystals (storage cap **60**) and Medallions (cap **6**), with currency stacking to 5,000. Temple runs end at Atziri, and Atziri's Vault drops the new Orb of Sacrifice. The run is gated at area level 75. — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide); [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)
- **Runes of Aldur** (0.5 league): Runeforging with 4 outcomes: add **Runic Ward** to an item, replace defensive mods with a large Runic Ward pool, add new mods to Uniques, or upgrade low-level Uniques to deal far more damage. It adds 100+ new Runes and crafting currencies and new gems that consume Runic Ward instead of mana. Completing **8 challenge tasks** unlocks a cosmetic armour set (Knight of Aldur). — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [search summary](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Runes_of_Aldur)
- **Essence, Strongboxes, Rogue Exiles, Azmeri Spirits** appear as map content and Fortress or Atlas-node modifiers (e.g. Jado increases unique strongboxes). — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- **Trials** (side endgame): the Trial of Chaos in 0.5.5 goes up to **Room 30**, players can leave between rooms, and rewards are now only Currency and Soul Cores. — [Maxroll 0.5.5](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-5-forbidden-rites-event-launch-date-and-endgame-changes)

### Inferences
- POE2's endgame is three overlapping progression meters: (1) **Atlas tree points**, a one-time, finite, fully allocatable meter from the Fortress; (2) **mechanic questlines → pinnacle keys**, deterministic first-clear then infinite farm; (3) **juice knobs** (Waystone mods, Tablets, Master options, Delirium %), a risk/reward dial. All three map cleanly onto idle-game systems: a finite "meta tree", "boss key" crafting from accumulated fragments, and a "difficulty multiplier" slider that raises both drop rate and fail chance.
- Self-reinforcing Tablet drops (running mechanic X yields more X tablets) are a **specialization flywheel**. In an idle game this could be a "focus" system where repeated farming of one zone raises that zone's resource yield.
- The "Quest version vs Infinite Farm version" split is a good template: guaranteed first-time narrative clears for casuals, and a farmable harder version for loot.

### Gaps
- No source gave a **Waystone tier → area/monster level table** for 0.5 (historically T1 ≈ area level 65, but unverified for 0.5).
- Exact pinnacle drop tables (Arbiter of Ash/Divinity, Xesht, Kulemak, Atziri) were not found.
- The full list of Tablet types and their stat ranges for 0.5 was not found.
- The Arbiter of Ash access conflict (3 keys vs 2 Crisis fragments) is unresolved; it is possibly a 0.5.0 vs later-patch difference.

## 3. Death penalty / experience loss and difficulty scaling

### Takeaway
Dying in endgame maps costs **10% of the XP needed for the next level** (no level-down) and consumes one map attempt (portal/revive). Running out of attempts closes the map and forfeits its objectives. Pinnacle bosses don't cost XP, but a wipe burns the key. Difficulty scales through Waystone tier (T1→T15/16), mod count (up to 6, which also reduces revives), Master and Doryani empowerment, Delirium % chains, Trial room depth (now 30), and pinnacle "farm" versions.

### Cited Findings
- Endgame maps: death = **−10% of XP toward next level** and uses one portal; when portals run out the map closes permanently. — [ApolloTrail (0.5)](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide). When all portals are lost "the node's extra objectives are gone and it must be repeated." — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- Revive count: older guides say the base is **5 retries on a Normal/Magic Waystone, dropping to 0 if the Waystone has 6 modifiers**, and that pinnacle fights cost no XP but have limited revives. — [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Path-of-Exile-2/archives/494914); [GameRant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-death-penalty-what-happens-die-dying-effects-poe2/) (both 400–650 days old, so possibly OUTDATED). A 0.5-era guide says 6 modifiers reduce portals to **1**. — [search summary of poe2hub/Maxroll](https://poe2hub.net/mechanics/atlas-waystones-towers/). These sources conflict.
- Doryani (Master) can grant "an extra revival" on Waystones. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping). In 0.5, fallen party members can be revived quickly after the boss is defeated. — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- Campaign: early guides report no XP loss in Acts 1–5 and 5% in Acts 6–10 (the old Cruel structure). — [search summary of Game8/GameRant](https://gamerant.com/path-of-exile-2-death-penalty-what-happens-die-dying-effects-poe2/). **OUTDATED**, since Cruel acts were removed in 0.3. Dying in Trial of the Sekhemas or Trial of Chaos restarts the trial. — same source
- Hardcore: "Hardcore Runes of Aldur is parented to Standard." If a character dies, it continues in Standard (permadeath for league purposes). — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- Pinnacle attempts are limited and a wipe burns the key or fragments. A forum complaint says "a player should not lose multiple hours of RNG progress on entry keys after a failed pinnacle boss attempt." — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide); [Official forum [COMMUNITY]](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3895325)
- Defensive thresholds recommended to start mapping: 75% Fire/Cold/Lightning resist overcapped by 15–20%, and Life+ES above 2,000. Map mods can reduce max resists, add monster damage or prevent leech. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide)
- Scaling knobs: each Waystone mod raises difficulty and loot. Delirium chains add +10% per map up to 200%. The Trial of Chaos goes to Room 30. Doryani raises mod effectiveness. — [Maxroll](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping); [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540); [Maxroll 0.5.5](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-5-forbidden-rites-event-launch-date-and-endgame-changes)

### Inferences
- POE2's penalty creates **loss aversion on two currencies at once**: time (XP progress) and consumables (Waystones/keys). An idle game can soften this. For example, a failed boss attempt could refund part of the key, or an "XP debt" could be repaid by future gains instead of deleting progress. That avoids the main complaint (losing hours of keys) while keeping stakes.
- The "more mods = more loot but fewer lives" coupling is an elegant single-slider risk/reward mechanic that idle games could use directly, e.g. "Danger level" sets revives, monster HP and loot quantity together.

### Gaps
- The exact 0.5 revive count per Waystone (5 vs other) and the mod-count → revive mapping are unverified.
- Whether XP loss changed at any point in 2025–2026 is unverified. No official note found.
- The original 0.1 one-portal rule and when it changed were not confirmed.

## 4. Trade economy (trade site, Currency Exchange, async Merchant, money currencies, ratios, gold, Standard/SSF, bots/RMT)

### Takeaway
POE2 runs a **three-layer player economy**: (1) the **Currency Exchange**, an asynchronous order book for stackables that charges a gold fee and has existed since EA; (2) **asynchronous item trading** since 0.3, where items listed in a paid Merchant Tab sell instantly via the in-game Market or the trade website while the seller is offline; (3) the legacy **trade website + whisper** flow. **Divine Orb** is the store of value and big-ticket price unit. Exalted Orb is the everyday "loose change", which inflates hard over a league (from about 85–130 Ex per Divine early to 400–790 Ex later). Chaos Orb sits in between. **Gold** is non-tradeable and serves as the fee and sink currency. Every league offers Softcore trade, Hardcore and SSF variants; dead or expired characters roll into Standard.

### Cited Findings
**Currency Exchange**
- Pre-launch, GGG described a currency exchange "so folks can exchange currencies with other players for a gold fee." Maxroll's Dec 2024 guide describes it as "an asynchronous trading system where you can list various currencies, crafting items, and various atlas mechanic related items." **Gold** is "used to purchase items from vendors, list currencies on the exchange, and reallocate your passives… it CANNOT be traded to other players." — [Maxroll trade guide](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/trade-in-path-of-exile-2) (so it has been present since EA launch, 0.1)
- Mechanics: players pick have/want, the system suggests a market-based rate, matched orders fill instantly, and unmatched orders stay pending. Gold fees scale with quantity and the rarity of the items exchanged. Cancelling refunds the currency but **not** the gold fee. Only stackables (currency, crafting materials, fragments) are supported. — [search summary of third-party guides: nexttier.pro / pvpbank / conquestcapped](https://nexttier.pro/guide/poe2-trade-guide) (exact gold-fee formula not verified)
- The exchange NPC was originally Alva; in 0.3 the function reportedly moved to **Ange** (Act 4 town / hideout), with Alva's stored items still accessible. — [aoeah [COMMERCIAL]](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4114--poe-2-03-asynchronous-trade-system--currency-exchange-guide). Sources conflict on the current NPC.
- 0.5.0 removed certain items from the exchange (Black Scythe, Broken Circle, Order and Sun Artifacts, Exotic Coinage, Petition Splinter, Runic Splinter). Some Omens appear on the exchange **only in Standard**. — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- Flipping (market-making) on the exchange is a recognised money-making activity with its own Maxroll guide. — [Maxroll flipping guide](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/flipping-with-the-currency-exchange)

**Asynchronous item trade (0.3+)**
- Added in **0.3.0 (29 Aug 2025)**. Sellers need at least one **Merchant Tab** (bought from the shop, or upgraded from a pre-0.3 Premium tab on the website) and price items in any currency. Listed items lock for a cooldown. Buyers use "The Market" panel in-game or the official trade site, purchases deliver instantly to the hideout, and the seller can be **offline**. Proceeds collect in Ange's Earnings Tab. Free-to-play accounts unlock Ange later, after Act 4 islands and the Shoreline Hideout (about 2–3 h more than supporters). Premium quad tabs don't work for selling. — [aoeah [COMMERCIAL]](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4114--poe-2-03-asynchronous-trade-system--currency-exchange-guide); [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/18216-path-of-exile-2-third-edict-patch-notes-0-3-0-update-sprinting-act-4)
- 0.5 QoL: "Native item price checking" was added. Shift-Alt click on an item opens trade-search filters, and for Rares individual mods can be toggled to see their price effect. — [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)

**Money currencies and ratios**
- **Divine Orb** rerolls mod values (drop level 35, rarer than Exalted) and is the "de-facto premium currency"; wealth is stored in Divines. **Exalted Orb** adds a mod to a Rare (drop level 5, about 0.05% base monster drop) and is "loose change" that loses purchasing power over a league. **Chaos Orb** removes 1 mod and adds 1 (not a full reroll) and is also used as a trade unit. — [timesaver [COMMERCIAL]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-divine-vs-exalted-orb-guide)
- 0.3-era reference rates: 1 Mirror of Kalandra = 18k Chaos = 330 Divine; 1 Divine = 57 Chaos = 85 Exalted; 1 Perfect Exalted = 147 Chaos; 1.6 Ex = 1 Chaos. — [aoeah [COMMERCIAL]](https://www.aoeah.com/news/4114--poe-2-03-asynchronous-trade-system--currency-exchange-guide) (OUTDATED, 0.3 era)
- Runes of Aldur softcore (poe2scout data): Exalted per Divine was ~709 on 7 Jul, ~675 on 8 Jul, ~502 on 13 Jul, ~431 on 15 Jul and ~427 on 19 Jul 2026. On 19 Jul, 1 Chaos ≈ 57 Ex and 1 Divine ≈ 7.5 Chaos. On 5 Oct 2026 (Forbidden Rites softcore), 1 Divine ≈ 740 Ex ≈ 10.8 Chaos. — [timesaver [COMMERCIAL]](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-divine-vs-exalted-orb-guide). Launch-period estimates of about 100–130 Ex per Divine and a 790 Ex figure on 7 Oct 2026 also appear on the same site. — [search summary](https://timesaver.gg/tools/poe-2/currency-price-check). The site contradicts itself (one page says about 65 Ex), so these are indicative only.
- 0.5.0 loot changes: "Divine Orbs are more common". Greater and Perfect currencies are rarer, and Transmutation and Augmentation are "significantly rarer". — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)

**League types**
- Runes of Aldur has Standard (softcore trade), Hardcore and **Solo Self-Found** versions with the same mechanics, plus private league versions. Hardcore is parented to Standard. — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- Expedition is disabled on Standard while the league is running and returns afterwards. — [ApolloTrail](https://apollotrail.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-endgame-guide); [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- The 0.5.5 event league is a "fresh economy reset" running parallel to Runes of Aldur. — [Maxroll 0.5.5](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/0-5-5-forbidden-rites-event-launch-date-and-endgame-changes)

**Bots / RMT / manipulation**
- An official-forum bug report says bots using search scanners buy desirable items faster than humans, distort prices, and crash the trade site "often on an hourly basis or more." Players report that listing a sought-after item for 1 Ex brings instant bot whispers. — [Official forum [COMMUNITY]](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3647762); [Steam [COMMUNITY]](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2694490/discussions/0/598513665563471020)
- A forum thread complains about currency-exchange manipulation by bots and currency sellers. — [Official forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3645497/page/4). Players blame RMT, guild farming and a lack of currency sinks for inflation. — [search summary of Steam threads](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2694490/discussions/0/598514342850243500)
- 0.5.0: a Temple of Atzoatl farming exploit "flooded the market with Divine Orbs" before an emergency nerf. — [EZG blog](https://www.ezg.com/blog/poe-2-patch-0-5-0-temple-of-atzoatl-exploit-massive-divine-orb-nerfs) (third-party, unverified against official notes)
- A visible RMT/boosting industry exists: many search results for PoE2 currency guides come from currency-selling sites (aoeah, timesaver, mmojugg and others). — observed across the searches above, e.g. [aoeah](https://www.aoeah.com/news/3680--path-of-exile-2-trading--currency-system--how-to-get-and-use-poe-2-gold-and-orbs)

### Inferences
- **Exalted inflation through the league** (≈85→740 Ex/Div) shows a faucet-heavy economy with weak sinks. Gold fees are the main hard sink, and gold can't be traded. For an idle game with a player market, a non-tradeable fee currency (gold) is the key anti-inflation and anti-RMT lever that POE2 already uses. A tiered "copper / silver / gold" currency ladder (Ex/Chaos/Divine) gives players a natural unit for every price band.
- The shift from whisper-trade to **offline async listings + order-book exchange** is the most idle-friendly part of POE2: trading no longer needs both players online. An idle game could start with async trade from day one.
- Seasonal resets with SSF/HC variants let different motivations coexist (competitive economy vs self-reliance vs permadeath). Idle "seasons" could use the same split.

### Gaps
- The exact gold-fee formula on the Currency Exchange and any listing limits are not found.
- No reliable (non-commercial) live poe.ninja snapshot was obtained; ratios come from a commercial site using poe2scout data.
- No official GGG statement on bot bans or RMT enforcement in 2026 was found.
- Whether Merchant-Tab trading changed for 1.0 / F2P is unknown.

## 5. Loot loop: farming strategies, loot filters, value distribution, session motivation

### Takeaway
The farm loop is: run Waystones → pick a mechanic focus (via Atlas tree + Tablets + Master) → bulk-sell stackables (currency, omens, fragments, Verisium, Desecrated currency) on the exchange → buy upgrades or chase pinnacle uniques. Value is extremely top-heavy: Divines and chase uniques (Mageblood, Headhunter, Mirror) make up most of a player's wealth, and many players report hours without a Divine. Loot filters (NeverSink/FilterBlade, 7 strictness levels) are essentially mandatory to hide low-value drops. Long sessions are driven by finite progression meters (Atlas points, questlines, pinnacle firsts, challenge cosmetics) plus the gamble of rare big drops.

### Cited Findings
- **0.5.4 farming tier list** (community-reported Divine/hour ranges, [COMMERCIAL] source): S = Grand Expedition (Runes of Aldur) 10–20 div/h (50 is the optimised ceiling); S = Vaal Temple → Atziri 3–8 (very low investment, gated at area level 75); A = Ritual 4–12; A = Abyss 4–10; B = Map juicing with Tablets/Towers 6–20 if sustained (high investment); B = Breach 3–7; C = Delirium 3–9 ("spiky"). Valuable bulk commodities include Liquid Verisium, the Orb of Sacrifice (from Atziri's Vault), Omens, Desecrated currency and fragments. — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)
- Exalted base drop is about 0.05% per monster; Divine is rarer (exact rate unpublished). — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-divine-vs-exalted-orb-guide)
- Player reports of scarcity: one 0.5.5 forum poster got a single Divine across a long stretch of maps ("0.5.0 was exactly the same"). Another reports almost no currency at T6–T8 over 40–50 hours, while social media shows people holding hundreds of Divines. — [Official forum, Feedback on 0.5.5 [COMMUNITY]](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4005847); [Console divine drop thread](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3730148)
- Monster Effectiveness now gives half the previous quantity and XP bonus (0.5.0), and Catalysts drop only from the Genesis Tree. — [patch notes](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3932540)
- **Loot filters**: NeverSink's filter via FilterBlade has **7 strictness levels** (soft → uber-plus-strict), with semi-strict recommended for beginners. GGG supports online filter following. — [Maxroll FilterBlade](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/filterblade-launch-for-path-of-exile-2); [Maxroll filter support](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/loot-filter-support-for-path-of-exile-2); [NeverSink thread](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3693141). One guide suggests Regular for T1–T7, Semi-Strict for T8–T12, Strict for T13–T16 and Uber Strict for high-investment mapping. — [ExitLag](https://www.exitlag.com/blog/path-of-exile-2-loot-filter/) (third-party suggestion)
- Session motivators in 0.5: 5 storylines with clearer stopping points (games.gg), Fortress Atlas points (finite meter to 300+), pinnacle questlines, 8 challenge tasks for a league-exclusive armour set, and free uniques (Moment of Zen). — [games.gg](https://games.gg/path-of-exile-2/guides/poe-2-patch-05-return-of-the-ancients-rundown/); [Maxroll reveal](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/news/patch-0-5-return-of-the-ancients-reveal-summary); [Maxroll Atlas](https://maxroll.gg/poe2/resources/atlas-of-worlds-and-mapping)
- Chase: Ritual deferring and rerolling targets uniques like Mageblood and Headhunter. — [timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/poe2-0-5-4-currency-farming-tier-list)

### Inferences
- POE2's loot curve is a **power-law**: most drops are filtered noise, a few stackables give steady income, and rare jackpots (Divines, chase uniques, Mirror) drive excitement and social-media envy. An idle game can reproduce the dopamine of jackpots with offline-loot summaries highlighting the rare hits, and can *replace* the loot filter with auto-salvage tiers, which is effectively an automatic "strictness" setting.
- "Divines per hour" is the community's universal efficiency metric. An idle game could expose a similar visible **income-per-hour stat per farming strategy**, letting players optimise allocation (which zone or mechanic to idle in).
- Mechanic-specific farming with different investment/risk/variance profiles (low-variance Breach vs spiky Delirium vs high-investment juicing) is directly portable as idle "expedition types".

### Gaps
- No official drop rates for Divine Orb or chase uniques.
- No non-commercial verified Div/h figures. All income numbers are community estimates relayed by a currency-selling site.
- No data on average session length or playtime distribution.

### Inferences
- What players **love** and what is idle-translatable: deterministic goals layered on random loot (questlines, finite Atlas tree), meaningful build and crafting depth, and big jackpot moments.
- What players **hate** and what an idle game should avoid: progress deletion (XP loss, burned keys), opaque RNG with no pity, wealth inequality visible on social media, systems that demand micro-optimisation (Tablet juicing), and bot-dominated trade. Idle-game fixes could be **pity timers / bad-luck protection** for top currency, partial key refunds, and trade rate limits or async order books that make bot sniping pointless.
- POE2 shows that **slowing the game down** (0.2) triggered the strongest backlash. Idle players likewise react badly to retroactive nerfs of income rates, so balance changes should ideally land at season resets.
