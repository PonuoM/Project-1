# G5 roster expansion — 14 October 2026

This revision continues the original warm airship-and-forest world described in ../characters-g5/characters.md. Harbour guilds make brass-and-steel gauntlets and crystal-powered firearms, so the new equipment belongs to the same fantasy world. Forest summoners cultivate living wardens rather than borrowing an existing game's creatures.

## Updated roster

Ten characters: four melee, three mage, three ranged. Bramble's two weapon modes are one character with two sprite concepts.

- Melee: Mara / sword; Rika / fists (replaces Orrik's hammer slot); Sella / spear; Yara / dual daggers (new slot).
- Mage: Pipra / astrolabe wand; Vessa / spellbook; Noll / summoner staff (revised look).
- Ranged: Tavi / bow; Ilen / crossbow; Bramble / twin machine guns or giant laser cannon (replaces sling).
- The old hammer, sling and Noll concepts remain archived. No game integration or manifest changes.

## Rika Brassbeat — gauntlet brawler

Race: human. Class: melee / fists.
Personality: A cheerful harbour machine-fitter who fixes a stuck mechanism with one knock and a stuck argument with two.
Silhouette hook: two oversized boxy fists and a raspberry bob.
Weapon: paired brass-and-petrol clockwork boxing gauntlets, each about 9 art px. No hammer.

Palette:
- Hair / rose vest: #72243d / #b63d59 / #e77687
- Petrol gauntlet inserts: #193e44 / #32666e / #6e9a9c
- Skin, brass, charcoal and leather use shared ramps.

Moves:
1. Dockside One-Two: forward jab then heavy rear-hand straight, with short whole-body recoil.
2. Rivet Uppercut: tiny crouch, rising fist, brief pause at impact, grounded recovery.
3. Mooring Breaker: both gauntlets brace together, one committed forward body blow.

Reference translation: oversized mechanical punching weapons and confident energy. Original costume, hair silhouette and harbour-guild equipment; no copied emblem.

Recommended: melee-brawler-v1.png.

## Yara Veilstep — dual-dagger ninja

Race: human. Class: melee / assassin.
Personality: A quiet theatre roof-runner who leaves a knotted red thread wherever she keeps a promise.
Silhouette hook: high tied ponytail, trailing short scarf, half-mask and two separated crescent blades.
Weapon: two short curved daggers, roughly 10 art px each. One forward, one low behind.

Palette:
- Purple-black hair: #292632 / #474052 / #746580
- Plum wrap tunic / scarf: #382139 / #62364f / #956078
- Lavender wraps: #51445e / #82718c / #b5a4bf
- Red cord: #6a242c / #a83c45 / #d87778
- Skin, charcoal, brass and steel use shared ramps.

Moves:
1. Thread-Cross: alternating left/right low cuts with clean, separate blade arcs.
2. Roofshadow Step: vanish-sized dash past the target, one backhand return slash.
3. Twin Crescent: crossing double cut followed by a planted backward hop.

Reference translation: tied hair, wrap clothing, stealth posture and dual blades. The blades stay small enough for short chibi arms.

Recommended: melee-assassin-v1.png.

## Bramble Kettlehop — round-eared gunner

Race: small round-eared beast-folk, preserving the owner's selected character. Class: ranged / gunner.
Personality: A pantry courier and eager tinkerer who carries biscuits, spare gears and far too much firepower.
Preserved identity: shaggy tan giant head, pink round ears, cream face, red vest, cream sleeves, grey trousers, brown boots and backpack.
Silhouette hook: round ears and backpack; two tiny guns versus one comically oversized cannon.

Palette:
- Tan hair / fur: #8a5a36 / #b57a4c / #d99c6c
- Ear inner pink: #b56b58 / #e0987d / #ffcab0
- Cream face / sleeves: #b5a48b / #d9c7ad / #f2e3cc
- Apple vest: #782222 / #a83232 / #d14b4b
- Slate trousers: #474a52 / #686d78 / #9197a6
- Cyan crystal / laser aperture: #126a79 / #12b4c4 / #8be8ef
- Brass, steel and leather use shared ramps.

Weapon modes:
- Twin clockwork machine guns, one per hand, compact brass drum magazines and steel barrels. File: ranged-gunner-dual-v1.png.
- Giant crystal laser cannon, held in both hands at waist, rear grip and underside support, solid cyan front aperture. File: ranged-gunner-laser-v1.png.
These are alternative loadouts for the same character; not three simultaneous guns.

Moves:
1. Biscuit Burst: alternating short machine-gun bursts, asymmetric recoil and quick reset.
2. Gearshift Swap: tuck both pistols, lift heavy cannon, plant feet; potential weapon-switch animation.
3. Pantry Overload: cannon charging anticipation, single straight laser burst, strong backward recoil and longer recovery.

Reference translation: a small charismatic gunner with oversized equipment. The selected character is retained; no raccoon recolouring or copied franchise armour.

Recommended: both v1 weapon variants.

## Noll Brackenbell — forest summoner

Race: plant-and-stone folk. Class: mage / summoner.
Personality: A patient keeper of forest paths who names his summoned wardens after travellers he remembers.
Silhouette hook: broad wooden crown, branching twig antlers, moss beard, leafy shoulder mantle and upright staff.
Weapon: wooden fork staff encircling a turquoise seed crystal with a hanging acorn. Forward hand visibly wraps the middle shaft; shaft extends both above and below the hand.

Palette:
- Stone face / hands: #757873 / #9da19a / #c5c9c1
- Wooden crown / bark clothes: #593122 / #925c32 / #d39a53
- Leaf mantle / root boots: #3d4827 / #75823e / #a9b65d
- Moss-blue beard: #294e48 / #487e71 / #82ad93
- Seed crystal: #236b68 / #3aada5 / #93d9c9
- Gold acorns: #946c22 / #c99836 / #f0c259
- Leather and wood use shared ramps.

Moves:
1. Seedward Call: a short staff tilt and seed gesture summon a small root-and-pebble guardian ahead.
2. Root Companion: staff butt taps the ground; the guardian travels under the soil and surfaces near the target.
3. Grove Pact: Noll braces the staff vertically while a larger branch warden performs one heavy attack, then dissolves into seeds.

No companion is baked into the idle sprite; summons should be separate later animation assets. The brief requested a summoner look and centre grip, not additional pet sprites.

Recommended: mage-summoner-v1.png.
