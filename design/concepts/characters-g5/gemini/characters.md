# Idle Loot — Playable Character Roster Concepts (G5)

## World Overview: The Wind-Step Archipelagos of Vaeloria

High above the Cloud-Chalk Sea, ancient stone kingdoms cling to terraced mountain mesas linked by brass-ribbed airship ferries, cable-gondolas, and wind-organ bridges. Vaeloria is a warm, lived-in storybook world where history is measured not by apocalyptic wars, but by the schedules of mail-zeppelins, the migrations of lantern-spores through moss-drowned forests, and the seasonal arrival of travelling stage-wagons in cobblestone market squares. Beneath the mist-shrouded cliffs lie the Salt-Reach Dunes, where colossal sandstone kilns and half-buried stone titans from an older age now serve as wind-shelters, pottery towns, and bell-foundries.

Across the kingdoms, many peoples share the same guild benches and tavern hearths: industrious humans who crew the sky-cranes and printing presses; long-eared hare-folk couriers who leap across wet slate rooftops; living kiln-born sandstone masons who hum like warm ovens; reef-dwelling coral sprite-folk who chart the currents by starlight; patient bark-and-pebble dryad wardens from the root-valleys; and nimble fox- and dormouse-folk scouts who patrol the windy ridgeways. Life here is adventurous, eccentric, and gently nostalgic—a world of brass compasses, ink-stained guild ledgers, warm bakery steam on cold moorland mornings, and grand theatrical rivalries.

- **Archer Favourite:** **`archer-1` (Tavi Saffronwind, Fox Beast-Folk Archer)** — The large curled fox tail behind the body balances the 20-art-px bow in front, giving the sprite a classic storybook adventurer silhouette that pairs naturally with Sella and Pipra.
