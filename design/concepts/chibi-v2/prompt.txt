Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: NEW art direction trial for a chibi pixel hero in a 2D side-scrolling fantasy idle game.
Create ONE exceptionally appealing low-resolution pixel character, standing facing RIGHT in a side / slight three-quarter side view. Full body, isolated, transparent background. This is a totally fresh design, not an iteration of the brown spiky-haired blue-and-silver knight.
Style: charming handcrafted 1990s Japanese fantasy game sprite, genuinely chunky visible square pixels, compact silhouette, clean strong pixel clusters, restrained 16-color palette, 1-source-pixel dark warm outline, flat two or three shade ramps, no detailed texture. Native design about 48 pixels tall inside a 64x64 cell, enlarged with nearest-neighbor to be easy to examine. Head almost half total height, small rounded torso, very short sturdy legs, tiny simple face with a single 2-pixel dark eye in profile and small cheek accent. Quiet confident personality. Hair is a simple soft dark-teal rounded bob with two or three big clusters, no spiky anime hair. Outfit: short warm ivory hooded shoulder mantle resting behind the head (face visible), muted moss-green tunic, terracotta sash, dark short boots, simple bronze wrist guard. A short broad sword held down at the right side, with an ivory blade and terracotta grip. The silhouette should feel like an adorable little wandering fantasy adventurer, not a heavily armored warrior.
Composition: ONE hero centered with generous transparent margins, no sheet, no extra poses, no parts chart, no text. The design must suggest detachable headwear, torso clothing, boots and weapon for future modular fashion; don't fuse weapon into body. Keep a clear gap around sword and feet. Favor shape, appeal and legibility over surface detail.
Avoid: big glossy anime eyes, realistic anatomy, long legs, silver plate armor, blue tunic, red scarf, spiky brown hair, high-resolution illustration disguised as pixel art, soft edges, blur, gradients, lighting glows, background, floor, shadow, labels, watermark. True alpha transparency.
